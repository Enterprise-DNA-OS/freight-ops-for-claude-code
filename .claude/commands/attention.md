---
description: Everything that wants a decision, worst first. Dangerous goods with the wrong driver, overloaded runs, work time breaches, expiring certificates, missing PODs, money asleep.
---

1. Run `npm run freight -- attention`.
2. The list is already ordered by how much each item can cost. Read it in that order and do not reorder it by ease:
   - **Dangerous goods on an unendorsed driver's run** outranks everything. If it is loaded, it comes off or the driver changes before the truck moves.
   - **An overloaded run and a work time breach** are offences already in motion, and the operator wears them, not just the driver.
   - **COF, rego and RUC clocks** stop the truck legally the day they run out; book them before they do.
   - **Late deliveries and freight stuck on no run** are the customer call you want to make, not receive.
   - **Missing PODs, unbilled deliveries, rate gaps, overdue invoices and drafts never sent** are the company's own money, asleep. A missing POD blocks its invoice.
   - **An undecided claim** is a customer relationship and a legal position rotting together.
   - **Over-limit exposure** is how a customer's bad year becomes yours.
3. For each item, say the one action: the command to run, the call to make, or the record to fix. Name who owns it.
4. Anything that needs a letter or an email is drafted, never sent: `npm run docs`, or write to `drafts/`.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
