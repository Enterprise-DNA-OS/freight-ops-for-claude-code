1. Run `npm run freight -- pods`.
2. Every row is money that cannot bill: the total blocked is the reason this list is the office's first job of the day.
3. For each row, the chase is the driver or the app for that run. When the signature is found: `pod <con> --name="who signed" [--at=]`.
4. A POD that genuinely does not exist is a conversation with the customer before invoicing, not a quiet invention. Draft that email to `drafts/`; a person sends it.
