---
description: The response letter for a cargo claim - accepted or declined - written from the record with the CCLA position stated, into drafts/. Never sent.
---

1. Run `npm run freight -- claim <no> --json` and `con <ref>` for the consignment behind it: what moved, what was signed, what the exception note says, what was decided and why.
2. Write the letter to `drafts/claim-<no>.md`: the consignment and delivery details, what the claim is for, the decision and its reason, and the money: the accepted amount, or the decline with grounds.
3. State the legal position plainly and only as far as the record supports: carriage at limited carrier's risk caps liability at $2,000 per unit (CCLA 2017 Part 5) unless the contract says otherwise. If the claim exceeds the cap, say what the cap is and what is offered. Nothing in the letter is legal advice; on a contested claim, say the operator should take advice.
4. Tell the operator the draft is ready and where. A person sends it.
